**NETFLOW**

Netflow tecnology is used to gather information about data flowing through a network, including who and what devices are in the network, and when and how users and devices access the network.

**PENETRATION TESTING**

It's known as **PEN TESTING**. it is the act of accessing a computer system, network or organization for security vulnerabilities.

**RISK MANAGEMENT**

It's the formal process of continously identifying and accessing risk in an effort to reduce the impact of threats and vulnerabilities.
