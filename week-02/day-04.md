**PROTECTING YOUR COMPUTING DEVICES**

Your computing devices are the portal to your online life, storing a lot of your personal data. These steps are important to protect the devices;

- Turn the firewall on.
- Install antivirus and antispyware.
- Manage your operating system and browser.
- Set up password protection.

**WIRELESS NETWORK SECURITY AT HOME**

Wireless networks allow wifi enabled devices such as laptops and tablets, to connect to the network by the way of a preasent network identifier, known as the service set identifier [SSID].

**WHAT IS ENCRYPTION?**

Encryption is the process of converting information into a form which unauthorized parties cannot read it.

**TERMS OF SERVICE**

It's a legally binding contract that governs the rules of the relationship between you, the service provider and others who use the service.

**OPEN AUTHORIZATION**

The open authorization [OAuth] is an open standard protocol that allows you to use your credentials to access third-party applications without exposing your passwords,
